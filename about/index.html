I'm Ashlon.

A product designer based in New York.

I got into product design by trying to build a startup while studying product innovation at Carnegie Mellon. The startup didn't take off; the case study got me hired.

I've been a designer, a PM, and sometimes somewhere in between. That path has taken me across patented ideas, 0-to-1 product bets, and end-to-end product redesigns.

Today, I'm at Rove, where our mission is to help people travel the world for free. Check it out, it's pretty cool.

Before product, I was an architect and urban designer. Architecture taught me that no decision exists in isolation. Move one thing and you might affect structure, engineering, materials, cost, or the experience of the whole space.

That's shaped how I build products.
